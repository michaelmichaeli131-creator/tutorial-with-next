# Blast Rush Arena — Engagement and Virality Design

## Design goal

The game should create mastery, surprise and social stories without relying on manipulative energy timers or pay-to-win pressure.

## Core loops

### 20-second loop

Read the field, choose the correct target, create a chain, protect the reactor and preserve the combo. The player always has a visible near-term decision.

### 3-minute loop

Complete directives, adapt to a stage mutation, select an augment and defeat a multi-phase boss. A run should continuously change its tactical texture.

### Multi-session loop

Gain XP and Shards, improve personal records, finish daily missions, climb the leaderboard and send challenges to specific friends.

## Viral loops

### Live room invite

A player creates a private room and shares a URL containing the room code. The recipient lands directly on the Join flow, reducing friction.

### Asynchronous score challenge

At the end of a strong run, the player shares a target score and Seed. The friend can respond later, even if the creator is offline. The result shows the exact score gap and rank among attempts, encouraging another share or retry.

### Rematch loop

A live duel ends with one clear action: Rematch. Pressure attacks create memorable moments that players can talk about rather than producing a passive score race.

## Difficulty philosophy

- Difficulty increases through speed, composition and decision complexity, not only larger health values.
- Each stage teaches one demand before combining it with others.
- Mutators are announced clearly so failure feels understandable.
- Directives reward alternative skills such as precision, chaining and survival.
- Duel reactor failure causes a score penalty and reboot instead of ending the match early, keeping both players active until the final second.

## Ethical retention

The system avoids loot boxes, artificial waiting, deceptive notifications and loss of purchased power. Engagement should come from competence, competition, expression and friendship.
