# Blast Rush Arena V6 — 2026 Product Research Mapping

## Signals used

### Retention has shifted toward live operation and repeatable goals

Sensor Tower's State of Gaming 2026 describes a mature mobile market where retention, events, community and live-operation depth matter more than relying on raw install growth. Its LiveOps research also highlights milestone rewards and tournament structures among high-performing games.

Implementation:
- visible wave directives and mutations;
- permanent progression and cosmetic Shards;
- Titan Gauntlet and daily missions;
- public leaderboard, rematches and asynchronous challenges.

### Multiplayer is increasingly social and asymmetric

Unity's 2025 gaming report found strong studio interest in multiplayer and asymmetric designs, including cooperative, social and lighthearted formats.

Implementation:
- Quick Match, public tables and private friend rooms;
- Core Volley creates asymmetric interaction: one player launches a payload while the other receives a high-risk, high-reward target;
- pressure attacks create tactical timing without stopping the opponent from playing.

### Cosmetic identity is safer than competitive monetization

The skin system is intentionally expressive rather than statistical. It changes reactor visuals, trails, lobby identity and Rival Core appearance, but never score, speed or health.

### Low-friction sharing matters

Direct room URLs, room codes, public table browsing, Web Share and asynchronous seeded challenges reduce coordination cost and let a friend respond later.

## Design choices deliberately rejected

- paid competitive power;
- loot boxes;
- artificial energy timers;
- streak-loss punishment;
- deceptive push notifications;
- hidden dynamic difficulty that changes rules after failure.
