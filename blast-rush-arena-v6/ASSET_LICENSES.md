# Asset and Audio Licensing

Blast Rush Arena V6 does not bundle third-party visual, music or sound-effect assets.

- Environments, cores, bosses, particles, beams and UI decoration are rendered procedurally with Canvas and CSS.
- Music and sound effects are synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API.
- The project therefore has no external runtime asset attribution requirement in this version.

Future asset packs should be added only with a documented commercial-use license. CC0 packs such as Kenney assets are suitable candidates, but no Kenney files are included here.
